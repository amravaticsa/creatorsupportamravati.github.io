Plan your project properly

What goes into your production quote?

Every project is different. These details help us quote accurately instead of promising one fixed rate for every shoot.

Shoot requirements

  • Shoot date, location and total hours
  • Number of cameras, crew and equipment
  • Travel, setup time and location permissions
  • Participants, products, props and shot list
  • Drone or special equipment requirements, subject to availability and applicable permissions

Editing & delivery requirements

  • Number and duration of final videos/photos
  • Vertical, horizontal or square formats
  • Captions, graphics, voice-over and language
  • Music licensing, B-roll and colour work
  • Draft deadline, final delivery and correction rounds

Pricing is quoted according to scope, crew size, equipment, travel, shoot hours, number of deliverables, editing complexity, graphics, voice-over, music licensing and revisions. Any extra work is discussed and approved before execution.

A smoother shoot day

Preparation makes better footage.

Before the shoot, confirm the purpose, audience, location, date, call time, participants, shot list, props, permissions and final formats. For interviews, prepare key questions and choose a quiet location. For product videos, clean the product and prepare all variants before the crew arrives.

✓Check battery, power, storage, focus, exposure and white balance.
✓Test microphone input and room noise; listen with headphones.
✓Review critical shots before leaving the location.
✓For long sessions, confirm recording limits, backup power and file transfer in advance.
Plan · Test · Record · Review
Editing and file handover

Clear feedback. Organised delivery.

To keep revisions efficient, share consolidated notes with timecodes—for example, “00:18 replace this shot” or “00:32 correct the product name.”

What to send for an editing quote

  • Raw footage duration and file size
  • Desired final length and platform
  • Reference style and number of videos
  • Caption, graphics, language and music needs
  • Deadline and whether footage is already recorded

Final review checklist

  • Check spelling, phone numbers and addresses
  • Verify logos, captions and brand details
  • Confirm music and supplied assets are properly licensed
  • Keep original files unchanged and organise footage by project/camera
  • Approve the final export in the agreed formats
Frequently asked questions

Before booking a production project.

Can I give you footage that is already recorded?

Yes. CSA accepts editing-only enquiries. Share the raw footage duration, desired final length, platform, reference style and deadline for a quotation.

Can CSA handle both shooting and editing?

Yes. You can discuss a complete shoot-and-edit assignment. The shoot hours, crew, equipment, deliverables and correction rounds are confirmed in writing.

Do you shoot product videos, business promotions and Reels?

Yes. Production can include product/profile shoots, brand awareness films, short-form Reels, YouTube content, podcasts, interviews and event highlights.

Can you cover events or provide live video support?

Event coverage and live production support can be discussed based on date, venue, duration, technical requirements and team availability.

Are drone shots included?

Drone work is quoted separately and depends on operator/equipment availability, location restrictions and applicable permissions and rules.

How many revisions are included?

The number of correction rounds and what counts as a revision are confirmed in the project quotation before editing begins.
