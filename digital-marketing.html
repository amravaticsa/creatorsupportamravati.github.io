Digital Marketing

Digital marketing support including SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads and Google Business Profile marketing.

How we can help

We plan digital campaigns around your business goals and audience. Our support can include campaign planning, Google Ads, Meta advertising setup, search visibility and Google Business Profile marketing.

Project planning

Campaign scope, platform access, creative requirements and reporting expectations are discussed before work begins.

Digital marketing that starts with a business goal

Digital marketing is not simply boosting posts or running ads. Before selecting a platform, a business should know which audience it wants to reach and what action it wants that audience to take: call, visit a shop, request a quotation, book an appointment or learn about a service. CSA helps plan the campaign around that goal.

Campaign and channel support

  • Google Ads: discuss search intent, service areas, landing pages, keyword themes, campaign structure and conversion measurement before launch.
  • Meta Ads: plan audience, creative format, message, campaign objective and enquiry flow for Facebook and Instagram placements.
  • SEO foundations: improve page titles, headings, service explanations, internal links and useful information that helps visitors understand an offer.
  • Google Business Profile: review business information, categories, service details, photos, hours and customer enquiry pathways.

What we need from a client

Share your business location, service area, ideal customer, best-selling services, website or profile links, approximate budget and the action you want customers to take. If you have previous campaign data, it helps identify what has and has not worked.

How performance should be reviewed

Do not judge a campaign only by likes or impressions. Depending on the objective, review calls, form submissions, qualified enquiries, cost per lead, landing-page behaviour and the quality of incoming enquiries. Tracking should be configured before launch wherever possible.

Important expectations

Advertising platforms change and results depend on competition, offer, budget, location, creative quality and website experience. CSA does not guarantee sales, leads, rankings or a fixed return. Ad spend is separate from service fees unless a written quotation says otherwise. The client retains ownership of their advertising accounts and should provide access through the platform's official permissions.
