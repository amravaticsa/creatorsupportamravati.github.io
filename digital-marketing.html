Frequently asked questions

Before you start.

Does the advertising budget go to CSA?

No. Media spend is normally paid directly to Google or Meta through the client's advertising account. CSA's service fee is quoted separately unless a written proposal states otherwise.

Can CSA manage both Google Ads and Instagram/Facebook Ads?

Yes. The recommended channel mix depends on your customer, offer, location and objective. We can scope Google Ads, Meta Ads or a combined plan.

Do you guarantee leads, sales or ranking?

No. Marketing outcomes depend on multiple factors outside any agency's control. We agree on the work, measurement approach and review process, but do not promise a fixed result.

Do I need a website to run ads?

Not always. Some campaigns can use a platform lead form or an approved messaging route. However, a suitable landing page often helps explain the offer and build trust. We will recommend the practical route for your goal.

Will I own my ad accounts and business profile?

Yes. Clients should retain ownership of their website, domain, Google Business Profile and advertising accounts. Access should be shared using official platform permissions.

Can I start with only SEO or Google Business Profile?

Yes. You can request a focused scope for local visibility, on-page SEO foundations or Google Business Profile support without taking a full advertising package.
