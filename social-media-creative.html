Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Do you manage and publish posts on our social media accounts?

The packages on this page are primarily for creative design and content planning. Account access, scheduling, publishing, community replies and full social media management can be added through a separate written scope.

Are photos and videos included in the packages?

Client-supplied photos and approved brand assets can be used. Professional photo/video shoots, stock assets with paid licences and video editing are quoted separately unless your proposal specifically includes them.

How many revisions are included?

Each design includes one consolidated revision round. Please send all requested changes together. New concepts, major direction changes or additional revisions may be quoted separately.

Can you create designs in Marathi, Hindi and English?

Yes. We can design in Marathi, Hindi and English based on the content supplied and approved by your business. Please confirm the language mix at the start of the project.

Do I receive editable source files?

Final exports are delivered in the agreed digital formats. Editable source files and working files are not included by default and can be discussed separately.

How do we start a monthly package?

Share your business details and preferred package. CSA will confirm the deliverables, schedule, payment terms and start date in writing before work begins.
