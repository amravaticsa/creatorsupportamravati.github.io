Social Media & Graphic Design

Social media management, graphic design, brand creatives and content planning.

How we can help

We help maintain a consistent digital presence through creative posts, campaign graphics, content calendars and social media support tailored to the brand.

Project planning

Posting frequency, platforms, content volume and approval process are defined in the monthly or project plan.

Consistent social content with a clear purpose

Social media works best when a business shares useful, recognisable information rather than posting only when there is a sale. A content plan helps balance product information, customer questions, proof of work and brand personality. CSA supports creative planning and design for businesses that want a more consistent presence.

Content categories to rotate

  • Explain: answer common questions about products, services, process, pricing factors or booking.
  • Demonstrate: show how a product works, how a service is delivered or what happens behind the scenes.
  • Build trust: introduce the team, share real project examples and publish customer feedback only with permission.
  • Inform: share opening hours, new arrivals, seasonal updates, events and service changes.
  • Invite action: make it easy to call, message, visit or submit an enquiry.

What a monthly plan can include

A monthly plan may define platforms, post formats, creative count, reel count, captions, publishing frequency, approval deadlines and reporting. The exact scope is agreed before the month begins. Daily posting is not automatically better; the schedule should match the business's capacity to produce and respond.

Brand consistency

Use a small set of brand colours, readable type, consistent logo placement and a repeatable visual style. Keep text large enough for mobile screens. Avoid cluttered creatives and make sure the main offer is immediately understandable. Use original or properly licensed photos, music and design assets.

Review what matters

Track saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, calls and qualified enquiries according to the goal. Reach and likes are useful context, but they do not always indicate business impact. Review the questions people ask and use them to plan the next batch of posts.
