Copyright Policy

Copyright and usage information for Creator Support Amravati website content.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Copyright & Intellectual Property Policy explains how rights in website content, creative work and client materials are treated by Creator Support Amravati (CSA). Rights in a particular project may be further defined in the accepted quotation, licence, release or written project agreement. Applicable copyright and other intellectual-property laws continue to apply.

1. CSA website and brand materials

Unless otherwise stated, the CSA name, logo, website design, original website text, graphics, layouts, illustrations, downloadable resources and other original materials published by CSA are owned by CSA or used under licence. Visitors may view the website and share links for lawful reference. Copying, republishing, adapting, distributing or commercially exploiting CSA-owned material requires prior written permission unless permitted by law.

2. Client-owned and client-supplied materials

Logos, brand names, product images, footage, photographs, scripts, music, fonts, testimonials, documents and other assets supplied by a client remain owned by that client or their relevant rights holder. The client grants CSA permission to use those materials only as reasonably necessary to quote, plan, produce, edit, publish or deliver the agreed project. The client must have the rights, licences and consents needed for that use.

3. Final deliverables and source files

Ownership, licence, permitted platforms, territory, duration, exclusivity and transfer of rights in final videos, photographs, podcast episodes, graphics, brand creatives, websites, code and other deliverables will be stated in the accepted project agreement. Payment of a project fee does not automatically transfer every underlying tool, template, stock asset, font, music track, plugin, software component or third-party licence. Raw footage, project timelines, editable design files, source code, working files and unused takes are included only when the agreement expressly says so.

4. Music, stock assets, fonts and third-party content

Music, sound effects, stock photos/video, fonts, plugins, software, platform libraries and other third-party materials remain subject to their own licence terms. A licence may restrict commercial use, paid advertising, redistribution, client transfer, territory, duration or use outside a particular platform. CSA will use or recommend such assets within the scope of the licence known to it, but the client should confirm that the intended use is covered where the client supplies or selects the asset. Platform-provided music may be subject to platform-specific restrictions and may not be licensed for use elsewhere.

5. Portfolio and promotional use

CSA may display a project, client name, logo, image, video excerpt or result in its portfolio, case studies or promotional material only where it has the client's permission, a contractual right or another lawful basis to do so. If the project agreement requires confidentiality or prohibits public display, that restriction will be respected. Clients who do not want public portfolio use should raise this before the project begins and have the restriction recorded in writing.

6. Website development and software components

Custom website code, design files, content-management configurations and documentation are handled according to the development agreement. Open-source software, frameworks, libraries, themes, plugins, APIs and hosted services remain subject to their respective licences and terms. Domain names and third-party accounts should be registered in the client's name where agreed; access credentials should be transferred securely and not published.

7. Permission requests and copyright concerns

For permission to reuse CSA material, or to report material that you believe infringes your rights, email amravati.csa@gmail.com. Include the relevant page or content, your contact details, a description of the work/right involved and the requested action. CSA may request reasonable information to assess the report and may restrict access to disputed material while reviewing a good-faith complaint.

Nothing in this policy transfers ownership of a client's or third party's intellectual property to CSA, or limits any right or exception provided by applicable law.
