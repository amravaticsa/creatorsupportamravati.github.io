Frequently asked questions

A few useful answers when planning live coverage.

Can you live stream a wedding or religious program?

Yes. We can discuss live coverage for weddings, ceremonies, bhajan, kirtan, pravachan, jagran and other programs. The setup depends on the venue, schedule and connectivity.

Can you cover an event with multiple cameras?

Multi-camera coverage and live switching can be planned for suitable events. Camera count, positions, crew and recording requirements are confirmed in the quotation.

Which platforms can you stream to?

YouTube Live and Facebook Live workflows can be discussed. Platform eligibility, account permissions, stream keys and current platform rules must be checked before the event.

Will you provide the full event recording?

Full recording can be included when requested. Please confirm the recording format, number of cameras and delivery timeline before booking.

Can you arrange LED screens, drone shots or internet backup?

These may be arranged or coordinated as separate requirements, subject to availability, venue permissions, legal requirements and the project scope.

How much does live event coverage cost?

Pricing depends on event duration, location, camera count, crew size, streaming requirements, equipment and deliverables. Contact CSA for a project-specific quotation.
