Production capabilities

More than just a camera recording

A well-planned live production brings together camera positions, clean audio, switching, graphics and a stable streaming workflow. The final setup is selected according to the venue, event duration, internet availability and budget.

  • Single or multi-camera coverage
  • Live camera switching
  • YouTube Live streaming setup
  • Facebook Live streaming support
  • Microphone and audio feed coordination
  • Presentation and speaker coverage
  • Lower-thirds, titles and basic overlays
  • LED screen / projector feed coordination
  • Event recording for later delivery
  • Short highlight and social media edits
  • Remote guest / online participation planning
  • Hybrid event production planning
