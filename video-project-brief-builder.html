What makes a useful video brief?

1. Start with the audience and goal

“Make a viral video” is not a measurable brief. Explain who needs to see it and what information or action matters to them.

2. Describe deliverables clearly

List the number of videos, lengths, aspect ratios, captions, language versions, thumbnails and file formats you expect.

3. Confirm assets and permissions

Identify who supplies footage, logos, locations, music and talent releases. Only provide material you own or are authorised to use.

4. Agree on feedback

Name the person who approves the work, how feedback will be combined and how many revision rounds are included in the quote.

5. Plan the schedule

Allow time for preparation, filming, editing, review, changes and final exports. A delivery date depends on receiving inputs and approvals on time.

6. Compare the full scope

Compare written deliverables, exclusions, usage rights, travel, taxes, payment milestones and handover terms—not only the headline price.
