Frequently asked questions

Things to know before booking.

Do you have fixed rental prices for every item?

Rental rates depend on the exact make/model, quantity, rental duration, date and accessories. Share your requirement and CSA will confirm the available options and quotation.

Can you help set up a podcast studio at home?

Yes. We can discuss room layout, camera angles, microphone placement, lighting, background and the equipment needed for your number of speakers and recording style.

Can I rent only one item?

Yes, you can enquire about an individual camera, lens, microphone, light, tripod or accessory. Availability and minimum rental duration, if any, are confirmed before booking.

Is pickup or delivery available?

Pickup or delivery arrangements depend on the equipment, location and booking schedule. These details and any applicable charges are confirmed in the quotation.

What if equipment is damaged or stops working?

Existing condition is recorded at handover. Report any issue immediately and follow the written rental terms for troubleshooting, damage, replacement and responsibility.

Do you sell equipment as well?

This page focuses on setup support and rental enquiries. If you need to purchase equipment, share your use case and we can discuss suitable specifications; availability and purchase arrangements are separate.
