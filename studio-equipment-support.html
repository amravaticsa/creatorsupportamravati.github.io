Practical setup support for creators and small studios

A useful recording space does not need to be complicated. The right equipment depends on what you record, how many people appear on camera, the room size and whether the content is live or edited later. CSA can discuss home and small-studio layouts and equipment rental requirements.

Start with the recording use case

  • Talking-head videos: stable camera or phone mount, soft key light and a microphone close to the speaker.
  • Two-person podcasts: separate microphones, headphones, suitable audio interface or mixer and clear camera framing for both speakers.
  • Product videos: stable tabletop, controllable light, clean background and close-up lens or camera position.
  • Online classes: reliable power, clear voice, screen capture or presentation setup and a repeatable camera angle.

Equipment categories

Rental enquiries may include cameras, lenses, tripods, microphones, lights and teleprompters. The exact make, model, quantity and dates must be confirmed before a booking is accepted. Do not assume a particular item is available until CSA confirms it.

Before renting equipment

Tell us the shoot date, duration, location, intended use and the equipment you already own. Confirm pickup or delivery arrangements, deposit if applicable, return time, accessories included, battery or power needs and what to do if equipment is damaged. Test all items at handover and report any issue immediately.

Basic room setup tips

Place the subject away from a bare wall if space allows, soften harsh light, reduce echo with suitable soft furnishings and keep cables secured away from walkways. For interviews, test both microphones separately and monitor with headphones. For long recordings, plan power, storage and heat management before the session.

Availability and responsibility

Equipment suitability and availability depend on the booking date and current inventory. Rental terms, security deposit, late return and damage responsibilities are confirmed in writing before handover. Ask for a setup consultation if you are unsure which equipment is appropriate for your room and content type.
