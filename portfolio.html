How we work

Creative production support

Every assignment starts with understanding the client, audience and purpose of the content. The production plan is shaped around the brief and the deliverables agreed for that project.

01 · Planning

Discuss the objective, audience, key message, format, location and publishing requirements before the shoot.

02 · Production

Coordinate the shoot and capture the required footage, interviews, visuals or podcast material according to the project brief.

03 · Editing & delivery

Organise the footage, edit the agreed content and prepare the final files in the formats planned with the client.
