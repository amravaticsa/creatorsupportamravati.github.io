How to choose the right service

Start with the outcome you need. If people do not understand your offer, a website or brand explainer may help. If you need regular social content, a planned shoot and editing package may be more useful than a one-off video. If your business already has content but needs more relevant enquiries, discuss audience, landing page and campaign setup before spending on ads.

Project process

  1. Share your business, target audience and goal.
  2. Discuss scope, deliverables, timeline, location and budget.
  3. Approve the quotation and production plan before work starts.
  4. Review drafts and provide consolidated feedback within the agreed revision scope.
  5. Receive final files or campaign handover as agreed.
