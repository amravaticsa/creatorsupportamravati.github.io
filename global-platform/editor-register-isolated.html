Registration and verification process

1. Submit profile

Add your location, languages, editing skills and up to three video work samples.

2. Identity / KYC check

Upload one government-issued ID through the secure form. KYC documents stay private and are never shown to clients.

3. Profile and sample review

CSA separately checks your skills, experience, rights confirmation and each portfolio video.

4. Account approval

Only KYC-verified and profile-approved editors receive account activation instructions and eligible project access.
