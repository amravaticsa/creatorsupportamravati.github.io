Highest-level restricted access

Super Admin Login

Secure access to the CSA Global control centre. Only the designated Super Admin account can enter this workspace.

Two-step secure sign-in

Sign in using your CSA User ID or registered email and password. A one-time verification code is then sent to your registered email address.

Open Secure Super Admin Portal

Never share your password or verification code. The portal checks your account role on the server; selecting a login link does not grant access.

Need help? If your account is not active or you cannot receive the verification code, contact the authorized CSA account administrator.
