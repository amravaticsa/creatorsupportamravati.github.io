How CSA Global works

Client submits a detailed project brief and budget.
CSA reviews the scope and sends an offer to suitable editors.
Editor accepts, reviews the agreement and completes the required deposit payment.
CSA confirms the assignment and shares only the files and information needed.
Editor submits work to CSA; CSA performs quality control before client delivery.
