FAQ

Before you book a podcast session.

Can you record a two-person or multi-guest podcast?

Yes. We can plan a setup for solo hosts, interviews and panel conversations. Camera count, microphone requirements and crew are confirmed according to the format.

Can CSA shoot at our office or another location?

Location-based recording can be discussed. Venue, travel, available power, room acoustics and setup time affect the final quotation.

Do you provide only editing?

Yes. Share the original camera files, separate audio tracks, target duration and reference style so the editing scope can be assessed.

Can you create Instagram Reels from the full episode?

Yes. Short vertical clips can be included as a separate deliverable. The number, duration, captions and graphics are agreed before editing.

Do you upload episodes to YouTube or Spotify?

Upload preparation or publishing support can be discussed. Channel ownership and account access remain with you unless a separate management scope is agreed.

How do I get a quotation?

Share your preferred date, location, guest count, recording duration and required deliverables. CSA will discuss the setup and provide a project-specific quotation.
