Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I submit a video-editing project?

Use the CSA Global client account linked from this page. Submit your brief and project details there; the team reviews the scope and follows up with a quotation or next steps.

How is the editing quotation calculated?

The quotation depends on raw footage length, final duration, editing complexity, captions, motion graphics, audio cleanup, camera count, revision scope and deadline. CSA shares a written quotation before payment or editing begins.

How many revisions are included?

The included correction rounds and what counts as a revision are defined in the accepted quotation or project agreement. Consolidate feedback so the editor can apply it accurately.

How long are uploaded and delivered files retained?

The current page states that raw and delivered files may be removed after 60 days from project completion. Download and back up final files promptly, and contact CSA before ordering if a different retention arrangement is required.

Custom quotation & pricing

Every project is different. The quotation depends on raw footage length, final video duration, editing complexity, captions, motion graphics, audio cleanup, number of cameras, revision scope and delivery deadline. CSA will share a written quotation before payment or editing begins. No one-price-fits-all rate is assumed.

File privacy & delivery

Uploads and delivered files are stored in private CSA Global storage and are visible only to authorised users connected to the application. Only upload footage you own or are authorised to use. CSA may remove raw and delivered files after 60 days from project completion; download and back up final files promptly. Contact CSA before ordering if you need a different retention arrangement.
