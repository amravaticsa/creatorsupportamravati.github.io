Packages and pricing

Editing is quoted according to raw footage length, final video duration, complexity, captions, motion graphics, audio cleanup, number of cameras, revision scope and delivery deadline. We do not display a one-price-fits-all rate because project requirements vary. A written quotation will be shared before payment or editing begins.

File privacy and delivery

Uploads and delivered files are stored in a private bucket and are not publicly browsable. Your Order ID alone is not enough to access an order; keep the separate access token private. Only share footage you own or are authorised to use. CSA may remove raw and delivered files after 60 days from project completion; download and back up final files promptly. Contact us if you need a different retention arrangement before placing the order.
