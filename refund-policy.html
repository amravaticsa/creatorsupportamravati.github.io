Refund Policy

General information about cancellations and refund requests for CSA services.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Creator Support Amravati (CSA) reviews cancellation, rescheduling, refund and payment-adjustment requests for its creative and digital services. The accepted quotation, invoice or written project agreement may set project-specific conditions. This policy does not remove rights available under applicable consumer-protection or other laws.

1. Before paying or confirming a booking

Please check the service scope, deliverables, schedule, location, fees, taxes, advance and balance milestones, revision limits, third-party expenses and cancellation terms in the quotation. An enquiry or quotation request does not reserve a date. A booking is treated as confirmed only when CSA confirms it in writing and any stated booking payment or other condition is completed.

2. How refund requests are assessed

Each request is reviewed on its facts, including the amount paid, work completed, time or resources already committed, non-recoverable third-party costs, the reason for cancellation, the project agreement and applicable law. Where an amount is refundable, CSA will communicate the approved amount and process it through the original payment method where reasonably available, or another lawful mutually agreed method. Payment-provider or bank processing times may vary.

3. Video editing and post-production

For editing-only or shoot-and-edit projects, the review may consider footage received, file organisation, editing hours, completed drafts, revisions already delivered, licensed assets and other committed costs. If work has not started, the request will be reviewed against any booking or payment terms accepted for that project. If a draft or final edit has already been supplied, the completed portion and applicable agreement will be considered. A quotation request submitted through the Video Editing Orders page is not itself a paid order unless CSA separately confirms and collects payment.

4. Website development, design and digital services

For websites, landing pages, e-commerce, graphic design, branding, SEO, social media management and other digital services, the review may consider discovery, planning, design, development, content preparation, campaign setup, scheduled work and completed milestones. Domain registrations, hosting, premium themes/plugins, licences, stock assets, API charges and other third-party purchases may be non-recoverable once purchased or activated, subject to the supplier's terms and applicable law. Monthly retainers or subscriptions are handled according to the accepted plan and its billing period; unused work or partial months are not automatically refundable unless required by the agreement or law.

5. Advertising budgets and platform charges

Advertising spend paid to Google, Meta or another platform is separate from CSA's service fee unless the quotation expressly says otherwise. Amounts already spent, charged or committed by an advertising platform are subject to that platform's billing and refund rules and may not be recoverable through CSA. CSA will identify any unspent client funds or unperformed service portion in its review, where relevant and supported by the account records.

6. Shoots, photography, podcasts and event coverage

For a booked shoot, podcast session, photography assignment or event, the review may consider crew scheduling, preparation, travel, venue, permits, equipment and other costs already committed, as well as work completed. If a client requests a date change, CSA will try to accommodate it subject to team, venue and equipment availability. Weather, venue restrictions, event postponement, participant unavailability and other circumstances will be handled under the project agreement and applicable law. Any specific cancellation window or rescheduling fee must be stated in the accepted quotation.

7. Equipment rental and studio setup

Rental cancellation, security-deposit adjustment, late return, missing accessories or damage will be assessed against the written rental/hand-over record, equipment condition, rental period and agreed terms. Any deduction from a deposit should relate to documented charges, loss, damage or other agreed liability and applicable law. If CSA cannot supply the confirmed equipment, the parties may agree to a replacement, reschedule or refund of the affected amount, subject to applicable law.

8. Training, workshops and events

For training sessions or workshops, the review will consider the programme terms, seat or venue commitments, materials already supplied and whether the session has started or been delivered. Any transfer to another batch or date depends on availability and the conditions disclosed at booking.

9. Duplicate, failed or unconfirmed payments

If a payment appears debited but CSA has not confirmed it, do not pay again immediately. Contact CSA with the payment date, amount, transaction reference and Razorpay/payment ID if available. CSA will check the payment status with its records and provider. Duplicate or failed transactions will be reviewed for reversal or refund through the payment provider or bank process. Never send an OTP, UPI PIN or card security code.

10. How to request a review

Email amravati.csa@gmail.com with your name, registered phone/email, service, quotation or invoice number, payment reference, reason and relevant supporting details. You may also call/WhatsApp +91 92255 15679. CSA will acknowledge and review the request within a reasonable time and communicate the decision or any information needed. Any approved refund remains subject to payment-provider processing.

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive a statutory remedy or consumer right.
