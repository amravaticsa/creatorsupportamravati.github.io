Refund Policy

General information about cancellations and refund requests for CSA services.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This page describes the general approach to cancellations and refunds for Creator Support Amravati. The accepted quotation, invoice or written project agreement for a particular job takes priority where it contains specific terms.

Before a project starts

Please review the scope, deliverables, schedule, fees and payment milestones before approving a project. If you wish to cancel or change a booking, contact CSA as early as possible in writing.

Payments made through secure checkout

If you paid through the CSA secure payment page, include the Razorpay payment ID, quotation or invoice details and your reason when requesting a refund review. Any approved refund or adjustment will be handled under the accepted project agreement and applicable law. Payment-provider or bank processing times may vary and are outside CSA's direct control. Do not make a second payment merely because a confirmation is delayed; contact CSA with the payment ID so the transaction can be checked.

Work already started

Where work has started, any refund or adjustment will be assessed against work completed, costs already committed, third-party charges and the terms agreed for that project. Completed work and costs already incurred may not be refundable, subject to applicable law and the written agreement.

Rescheduling and cancellations

Rescheduling of shoots, events, rentals or other booked services depends on availability and any supplier or location costs. The applicable cancellation or rescheduling terms should be confirmed in the project quotation before payment.

How to request a review

Email amravati.csa@gmail.com with your name, project details, payment reference and reason for the request. CSA will review the request and respond using the contact details provided.

This policy does not limit any rights available under applicable consumer protection law.
