YouTube Video Editing Workflow: Raw Footage Se Final Export Tak

A practical workflow for organising footage, shaping a story, cleaning audio, adding B-roll and checking a YouTube export.

By: Creator Support Amravati · Published: 15 November 2026 · Category: Video Editing

A practical workflow for organising footage, shaping a story, cleaning audio, adding B-roll and checking a YouTube export. This guide is written for creators, educators and businesses who want to understand the process and prepare a clear editing brief.

Project brief

Editor ko topic, target viewer, language, final duration, platform aur deadline batayein. Tutorial, interview, vlog aur product explainer ka approach alag hota hai. Reference video style samjhane ke liye share karein, exact copy ke liye nahi.

Footage organisation

Camera-A, Camera-B, Audio, Screen-recordings, Slides, Logo aur References folders banayein. Original files preserve karein, backup rakhein aur upload se pehle clips play karke transfer verify karein.

Rough cut and story

Main points ko logical order mein rakhein. Repeated takes aur accidental pauses trim kiye ja sakte hain, lekin speaker ka meaning ya context change nahi hona chahiye. Tutorial ke steps ka correct order preserve karein.

Audio, B-roll and captions

Dialogue level consistent rakhein, clipping check karein aur noise reduction natural rakhein. B-roll explanation ko support kare. Captions readable hon aur names, figures aur technical terms manually verify kiye jaayen.

Export and review

YouTube long-form ke liye aam taur par 16:9 landscape aur Shorts ke liye 9:16 vertical use hota hai. Resolution source aur platform requirements ke hisaab se choose karein. Final export poora play karke sync, spelling, black frames, ending aur audio check karein.

Checklist

  • Opening mein topic clear hai?
  • Audio consistent hai?
  • Names, numbers aur captions correct hain?
  • Aspect ratio aur resolution sahi hain?
  • Music aur footage ke usage rights clear hain?
