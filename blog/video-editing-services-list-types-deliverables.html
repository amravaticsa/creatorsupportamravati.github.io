Video Editing Services List: Types, Deliverables & What Each Includes

Explore a practical list of video editing services for Reels, YouTube, podcasts, ads, events, interviews and business content.

By Creator Support Amravati · Video Editing Services List · Updated 15 November 2026

The phrase video editing services can cover many different tasks. Before requesting a quote, identify the format and deliverables you need. This list explains common editing services for creators, professionals and businesses, so you can describe your project clearly.

Short-form social video editing

Reels, Shorts and vertical clips often need a strong opening, tight pacing, subtitles, music, simple motion graphics and a 9:16 export. A batch of short videos may also need consistent branding and naming.

YouTube and long-form editing

Long-form edits may include story structure, removing pauses, multicamera cuts, B-roll, chapters, graphics, audio balancing and a 16:9 master. Confirm the target duration and whether the editor will also create short clips from the main video.

Podcast video and audio editing

Podcast post-production can include syncing camera angles, switching between speakers, cleaning dialogue, balancing microphones, adding an intro and outro, creating captions and exporting full episodes plus social snippets.

Business and brand videos

Brand films, service explainers and company profiles focus on message clarity. Typical tasks include selecting the best shots, arranging a narrative, adding logo and contact details, licensed music, titles and a final call to action.

Product and advertisement editing

Product videos and promotional ads may need detail shots, feature callouts, motion graphics, offer text, voice-over, music and multiple aspect ratios. Claims, prices and offer dates should be checked by the client before publishing.

Event and documentary edits

Event highlights, interviews and documentary-style videos involve footage review, story selection, audio cleanup, multicamera synchronisation and careful handling of names, dates and permissions.

Common post-production add-ons

  • Colour correction and creative colour grading
  • Dialogue cleanup, noise reduction and audio mixing
  • Subtitles, captions and translated text
  • Motion graphics, titles and logo animation
  • Thumbnail and poster design
  • Resizing and alternate platform exports
  • Short-form cutdowns from a longer master

What to include in your enquiry

Share raw footage duration, final video length, platform, aspect ratio, language, references, brand assets, deadline and expected number of revisions. Mention whether shooting is also required or whether you already have the footage. A detailed brief helps a provider quote accurately.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in basic video editing?

It commonly includes selecting clips, trimming, arranging, basic transitions and exporting. Captions, advanced graphics, complex audio work and stock assets may be separate.

Are video shooting and editing the same service?

No. Shooting captures footage; editing turns footage into a finished story. Some providers offer both, but confirm each scope separately.

Can one edit be used on every platform?

Not always. Different platforms and placements may need separate aspect ratios, lengths and safe text areas.
