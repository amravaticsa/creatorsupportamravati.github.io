AdSense-Ready Blog Kaise Banayein? Website Se Paise Kamane Ke Liye Har Article Mein Yeh 10 Cheezein Zaroori Hain

By Rajkumar Chavhan · Founder & Digital Marketing Specialist, Creator Support Amravati · Published / Updated: 28 November 2026 · 9-minute read · Blogging, SEO & Google AdSense

Google AdSense se website monetize karna chahte hain? Sirf kuch articles publish kar dena ya ad code add karna kaafi nahi hota. Aapki website par original aur useful content, clear navigation, transparent publisher information aur Google ki current programme policies ka compliance hona chahiye. Is guide mein aapko blog post ke essential elements ke saath poori website ki pre-apply checklist milegi.

Online blogging ke shuruaati phase mein ek common confusion hota hai: kitne articles hone chahiye, har article kitne words ka hona chahiye aur approval kitne din mein milega? In sawalon ka ek universal shortcut nahi hai. Google ki published eligibility guidance unique, high-quality content aur policy compliance par focus karti hai—kisi fixed word count ya universal article-count formula par nahi. Official AdSense eligibility requirements ko application se pehle zaroor padhein.

1. Google AdSense Kisi Website Ko Kaise Review Karta Hai?

AdSense activation ke dauran Google aapki site ko programme policies ke against review karta hai. Isliye sirf ek landing page ya ek article nahi, balki overall site experience, content quality aur policy compliance ko dekhna chahiye. Google ki eligibility guidance ke mutabik publisher ke paas apna unique aur interesting content hona chahiye, content policies follow karni chahiye aur site ke HTML source ko access karne ka adhikar hona chahiye.

Blogging mein E-E-A-T ka concept—Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness aur Trustworthiness—content quality ko evaluate karne ka useful lens ho sakta hai. Lekin ise AdSense approval ka koi official score, direct formula ya guaranteed checklist na samjhein. Google Search Central helpful, reliable, people-first content aur clear author/site background par zor deta hai.

  • Experience: Jahan relevant ho, practical examples, screenshots, testing notes ya first-hand observations share karein.
  • Expertise: Topic ko sahi, clear aur sufficient depth ke saath explain karein; uncertain claims ko fact ki tarah present na karein.
  • Authoritativeness: Author, publisher aur website ka purpose clearly batayein. Relevant references aur related resources dein.
  • Trustworthiness: Contact details, privacy information, transparent disclosures aur working navigation maintain karein.
Important: Koi fixed number of posts, minimum word count, traffic threshold ya author bio akela AdSense approval guarantee nahi karta. Google poori site aur account setup ko review karta hai.

2. Har AdSense-Conscious Blog Post Mein Yeh 10 Elements Rakhein

1. Clear aur problem-solving H1 title

Title reader ko bataye ki article kis sawal ka jawab dega. Main topic ko naturally title ke shuru ke hissa mein rakh sakte hain. Clickbait, exaggerated promises aur misleading claims se bachein.

2. Author name, date aur category

Article ke top par real author name, published date, substantial update ki date aur category dikhayein. Agar content expert-reviewed hai toh reviewer ka naam aur role bhi tabhi add karein jab review sach mein hua ho. Sirf date badal kar article ko fresh na dikhayein.

3. Useful introduction

Opening paragraphs mein reader ki problem, guide ka scope aur expected outcome explain karein. Long generic introduction, repeated keywords aur unnecessary self-promotion avoid karein.

4. Quick overview ya Table of Contents

Long guide mein linked TOC reader ko relevant section tak jaldi pahunchata hai. TOC ke labels section headings se match hone chahiye aur har anchor working hona chahiye.

5. Logical H2/H3 headings aur readable paragraphs

Topic ko meaningful sections mein divide karein. Short paragraphs, bullets, numbered steps aur descriptive headings use karein. Har 250–300 words ke baad heading add karna koi mandatory SEO rule nahi—heading tab add karein jab topic ka sub-section naturally shuru hota ho.

6. Original depth, examples aur practical steps

Sirf doosri websites ki information ko rephrase na karein. Apne examples, workflow, screenshots, calculations, limitations aur useful context add karein. 1,000 ya 1,500 words ka koi universal approval threshold nahi; article utna hi lamba ho jitna reader ke sawal ko properly solve karne ke liye zaroori hai.

7. Relevant original visuals, tables aur diagrams

Apne diagrams, screenshots, process illustrations ya properly licensed visuals use karein. Har image ke liye descriptive alt text likhein. Image decorative ho toh alt text ko unnecessary keywords se stuff na karein. Visuals content ko explain karein, sirf page ko lamba na banayein.

8. Contextual internal links aur related articles

Article ke relevant paragraphs mein apne website ke useful guides, tools aur service pages ko natural anchor text ke saath link karein. Reader ko next useful step milna chahiye; unrelated pages ki list ya keyword-stuffed anchors na banayein.

9. FAQs aur trusted outbound references

FAQs mein wahi genuine questions cover karein jo reader ko topic samajhne ya action lene mein help karein. Important policy, eligibility aur SEO claims ko Google ke official documentation jaise primary sources se support karein. External link sirf authority dikhane ke liye nahi, reader ko source tak pahunchane ke liye add karein.

10. Author bio aur editorial disclosure

Author bio mein actual background, relevant experience aur website se relation explain karein. Affiliate, sponsorship, product review ya commercial relationship ho toh clear disclosure dein. Agar article educational hai aur professional/legal/financial advice nahi hai, toh uski limitation ko bhi transparent rakhein.

4. Sirf Blog Post Nahi—Poore Website Par Yeh Trust Pages Rakhein

Ek strong article bhi weak site navigation, missing publisher information ya policy issues ko compensate nahi kar sakta. Website par ye pages clear, accessible aur updated hone chahiye:

  1. About Us: Website ka purpose, publisher/organisation kaun hai aur readers ko kis tarah ki information ya service milegi.
  2. Contact Us: Working email, contact form, phone ya genuine business contact details. Forms ko test karein.
  3. Privacy Policy: Actual data collection, cookies, analytics, advertising technologies aur third-party providers ke baare mein accurate disclosure. Apne setup ke mutabik policy maintain karein.
  4. Disclaimer: Content ki nature, limitations, professional advice boundaries aur relevant affiliate/sponsorship disclosures.
  5. Terms & Conditions: Website use, intellectual property aur applicable terms ko clearly explain karein.
  6. Editorial Policy: Content research, fact-checking, updates, corrections aur author/reviewer process explain karein—agar aap editorial standards publish karte hain.

In pages ko sirf footer mein rakhna enough nahi; links working hone chahiye, content genuine hona chahiye aur page par placeholder text nahi hona chahiye. Site ke har important section tak user ko clear navigation milni chahiye.

5. AdSense Apply Karne Se Pehle Practical Checklist

Is checklist ko ek baar page-level aur ek baar website-wide basis par review karein. Yeh practical quality-control workflow hai, Google ki official guaranteed approval checklist nahi.

CheckWhat to verifyDone when
Original contentArticles useful, accurate, non-duplicative aur reader-focused hain.Thin, copied, placeholder ya repetitive pages fix/remove kiye gaye.
NavigationHeader, footer, category links, breadcrumbs aur mobile menu work karte hain.Important pages 404 ya broken links par nahi jaate.
Trust pagesAbout, Contact, Privacy, Terms aur relevant disclosures accessible hain.Details actual business/site practices se match karti hain.
Article structureTitles, headings, byline, date, useful visuals aur relevant links.Each important article is readable and complete.
Technical SEOCanonical, sitemap, robots directives, mobile rendering and HTTPS.Search Console mein major crawl/indexing issues investigate kiye gaye.
Ad setupCorrect AdSense code and site ownership/connection setup.Code correct pages par installed hai; account setup tasks complete hain.
Policy complianceContent, ad placement, copyright and restricted content policies.Known violations resolve kiye gaye; accidental clicks encourage nahi hote.

Simple 5-step review workflow

  1. Content audit: Har published article kholkar check karein ki woh unique, useful, complete aur up to date hai.
  2. Navigation audit: Desktop aur mobile dono par header, footer, menu, categories aur internal links test karein.
  3. Trust audit: About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer aur Editorial pages ko proofread karein.
  4. Technical audit: HTTPS, canonical, sitemap, robots.txt, mobile layout aur Search Console coverage review karein.
  5. AdSense account review: Sites section mein site status, required setup tasks aur ad code check karke hi review request karein.
Traffic aur article count ke baare mein: Google ki published eligibility page koi universal minimum traffic ya minimum blog-post count specify nahi karti. Isliye “15–20 posts” ya “1,000+ words per post” ko official requirement na samjhein. Site ko genuinely useful banayein aur current policies follow karein.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Google AdSense approval ke liye website par kitne blog posts hone chahiye?

Google koi universal minimum post count publish nahi karta. Article count se zyada original, useful content, policy compliance, clear navigation aur complete site experience par focus karein. Bahut saare thin articles publish karne se quality problem solve nahi hoti.

2. Kya AI se likhe gaye blogs ko AdSense approval mil sakta hai?

AI-assisted content apne aap approval ya rejection decide nahi karta. Publisher ko original, helpful, accurate aur policy-compliant content dena chahiye. Raw, repetitive, misleading ya low-value content se bachein. AI se draft banaya ho toh facts verify karein, examples add karein aur human editorial review karein.

3. Kya Hinglish ya Roman Hindi mein AdSense content publish kar sakte hain?

AdSense supported languages ki current list check karna zaroori hai. Sirf language script dekhkar eligibility assume na karein; content language aur site content ko Google ki current supported-language guidance se verify karein. Reader ke liye language consistent, readable aur natural rakhein.

4. Kya AdSense approval ke liye website par traffic zaroori hai?

Google ki published eligibility requirements mein koi universal minimum traffic number nahi diya gaya. Unique, high-quality content, policy compliance aur site ownership jaise requirements ko meet karein. Real audience aur useful content par focus rakhein; traffic ko approval shortcut na samjhein.

5. AdSense site review mein kitna samay lagta hai?

Google ke mutabik review usually kuch din le sakta hai, lekin kuch cases mein 2–4 weeks tak lag sakte hain. Exact timing guaranteed nahi hai. Aap AdSense account ke Sites section mein status check kar sakte hain.

6. Kya Privacy Policy, About aur Contact pages zaroori hain?

Ye pages trust, transparency aur user experience ke liye strongly recommended hain. Privacy disclosures ko apne actual data collection, cookies, analytics aur advertising setup ke mutabik accurate rakhein. Google ke current policies aur local privacy requirements bhi review karein.
