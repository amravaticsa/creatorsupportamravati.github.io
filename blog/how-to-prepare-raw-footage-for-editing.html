Video Editing Ke Liye Raw Footage Kaise Prepare Karein: Upload Checklist

Prepare original camera files, audio, folders, project notes and usage permissions before sending footage to an editor.

By: Creator Support Amravati · Published: 15 November 2026 · Category: Video Editing

Prepare original camera files, audio, folders, project notes and usage permissions before sending footage to an editor. This guide is written for creators, educators and businesses who want to understand the process and prepare a clear editing brief.

Original footage preserve karein

Camera ya phone se original files copy karein; social media se downloaded compressed copy ko master footage na samjhein. Transfer ke baad clips play karke verify karein aur separate backup rakhein.

Folder structure

Video, Audio, Photos, Logo, Music-Voiceover, Script-Text aur References folders banayein. Multi-camera footage ko Camera-A aur Camera-B label karein. File names meaningful rakhein.

Audio and brand assets

External recorder ki original audio files, transparent PNG logo, brand colours, slides, product photos aur exact on-screen text attach karein. Music ya stock assets ke usage rights confirm karein.

Written editing brief

Audience, purpose, platform, final duration, tone, language aur deadline likhein. Important timestamps aur remove/retain notes add karein. Reference link style samjhane ke liye ho; copyrighted visuals ya soundtrack copy karne ke liye nahi.

Large file upload

Stable internet use karein aur upload complete hone ka confirmation dekhein. Interrupted upload ko complete assume na karein. Original master files delete na karein.

Privacy and permissions

Footage mein dikhne wale logon ki consent, music rights aur brand permissions verify karein. Unrelated private documents upload na karein. Private access token confidential rakhein.

Ready-to-send checklist

  • Original video files copied and tested
  • Separate audio tracks included
  • Logo, slides and approved text attached
  • Platform, aspect ratio and final duration stated
  • Deadline and deliverables confirmed
  • Usage rights and participant permissions checked
  • Backup retained by you

Example folder structure

Project_Name/\n  01_Camera_A/\n  02_Camera_B/\n  03_Audio/\n  04_Images_and_Logo/\n  05_Script_and_Text/\n  06_References/

Keep the folder names simple and avoid renaming files if that could break a camera's linked media structure. If you transfer a large project in multiple batches, tell the editor which batch is complete and whether any files are still uploading.

Before you share a file

Check that you own the footage or have permission to share it, especially when it contains customers, children, private conversations, workplace information or copyrighted music. Share only the material needed for the edit and use a private upload or access-controlled link where available.

Frequently asked questions

Should I send original footage or WhatsApp videos?

Whenever possible, send the original camera or phone files. Messaging apps may compress video and reduce quality.

How should I organise files for an editor?

Use separate folders for camera footage, audio, photos, logos, scripts, music and references. Label multiple cameras consistently.

What should I write in an editing brief?

Include the audience, purpose, platform, final duration, language, visual references, deadline, deliverables and timestamped instructions.
