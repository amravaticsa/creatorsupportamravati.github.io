India mein Video Editing Charges: Cost Factors aur Client Checklist

How to plan your editing project and request a clear quotation.

By: Creator Support Amravati · Updated: 14 November 2026

Video editor hire karne se pehle sabse common question hota hai: “Video editing ka charge kitna hoga?” Iska ek fixed answer nahi hai. 20-second Instagram Reel aur multi-camera 20-minute YouTube video ki editing effort alag hoti hai. Cost samajhne ke liye pehle project ka scope clear karna zaroori hai.

Video editing charges kin baaton par depend karte hain?

  1. Raw footage: Jitna zyada footage hoga, utna zyada time clips dekhne, select karne aur arrange karne mein lag sakta hai.
  2. Final video duration: Long video mein pacing, structure aur quality checks ka kaam badh sakta hai.
  3. Editing complexity: Basic cuts, multi-camera editing, motion graphics, compositing aur detailed colour correction alag level ke tasks hain.
  4. Audio work: Noise reduction, dialogue cleanup, separate audio sync aur sound design extra effort lete hain.
  5. Captions & graphics: Subtitles, animated titles, product labels aur branded templates scope ko affect karte hain.
  6. Deadline & revisions: Urgent delivery aur multiple correction rounds se timeline aur cost badal sakti hai.
  7. Output formats: Ek horizontal video ke saath multiple vertical Reels banana additional deliverable hai.

Alag-alag video projects ki requirements

Instagram Reels aur YouTube Shorts

Ismein strong opening, best moments selection, fast but clear pacing, readable captions aur vertical export important hote hain. Kaunsa platform aur desired duration hai, ye pehle batayein.

YouTube videos

Long-form editing mein footage organisation, story flow, audio balancing, B-roll, titles, basic colour correction aur final quality check shamil ho sakte hain. Talking-head tutorial aur multi-camera interview ka workflow same nahi hota.

Business promotional videos

Business video ko ek clear customer benefit communicate karna chahiye. Product/service visuals, staff ya location shots, voice-over, text overlays aur CTA ko planned sequence mein use kiya ja sakta hai.

Editor ko quotation se pehle kya details dein?

  • Raw footage ka link aur total approximate duration.
  • Final video duration aur platform—Instagram, YouTube ya website.
  • Style samjhane ke liye 1–2 reference videos.
  • Correct logo, brand colours, text aur names ki spelling.
  • Music/voice-over files aur unke usage rights.
  • Captions ki language, aspect ratio aur required versions.
  • Deadline, deliverables ki quantity aur final approval kaun dega.
  • Must-use shots aur woh footage jo include nahi karna hai.

Example: ek clear editing brief

Maan lijiye 25-minute interview record hua hai aur aapko 8–10 minute ka YouTube edit plus 3 vertical clips chahiye. Editor ko batayein ki camera aur external audio sync hain ya nahi, important sections kaunse hain, guest ka naam kya hai aur clips par separate captions chahiye ya nahi. Isse dono taraf deliverables clear rahenge. Ye sirf example hai, fixed package ya price nahi.

First draft review kaise karein?

  • Opening mein video ka topic jaldi clear ho raha hai ya nahi.
  • Headphones par dialogue level, clipping aur background noise check karein.
  • Names, numbers, logo aur on-screen text verify karein.
  • Phone par full export dekhkar captions aur framing check karein.
  • Resolution, file format aur aspect ratio platform ke hisaab se confirm karein.

Project delay se kaise bachein?

Footage ko clearly named folders mein share karein, ek consolidated feedback list dein aur final script/on-screen text approve karke bhejein. Multiple reviewers hon toh ek person feedback collect kare. Supplied footage, logo, music aur images use karne ki permission bhi confirm karein.

Video editing ka budget smartly plan karein

Sirf final video ki length dekhkar budget decide na karein. Raw footage kitna hai, camera angles kitne hain, audio cleanup, captions, motion graphics aur revisions chahiye ya nahi—ye sab editing effort badalte hain. Sabhi editors ko same brief bhejkar quotation compare karein.

Files editor ko kaise hand over karein?

Folders bana sakte hain: Camera A, Camera B, Audio, Logo & Brand Assets, Reference Videos aur Final Text. File names meaningful rakhein aur important moments ka short note dein. Raw footage ka backup apne paas rakhein. Final export phone aur laptop dono par check karein.

Frequently asked questions

Kya video editing ka charge per minute hota hai?

Kuch editors final duration ke hisaab se aur kuch project scope, footage volume ya complexity ke hisaab se quotation dete hain. Sirf per-minute rate nahi, usmein included work bhi compare karein.

Kya already recorded video edit karwa sakte hain?

Haan. Original footage, audio files, intended platform, desired duration aur reference style share karein, taaki material aur requirements check ki ja sakein.

Kya Reels editing mein captions aur music included hote hain?

Har package mein automatically nahi. Captions, music selection, sound effects, graphics aur revision rounds written scope mein confirm karein.
