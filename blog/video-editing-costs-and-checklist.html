Video Editing Charges in India: Cost Factors and Client Checklist

How to plan your editing project and request a clear quotation.

By: Creator Support Amravati · Updated: 14 November 2026

One of the first questions people ask before hiring a video editor is: “Video editing ka charge kitna hota hai?” There is no single price that fits every project. A 20-second reel made from organised clips is different from a 20-minute YouTube video with multiple cameras, detailed sound work, motion graphics and many revisions. The useful approach is to define the work before comparing quotations.

What affects video editing charges?

  1. Raw footage duration: More footage can mean more time spent reviewing, selecting and organising clips.
  2. Final video length: A longer edit may need more pacing decisions, structure and quality checks.
  3. Editing complexity: Basic cuts differ from multicamera editing, advanced motion graphics, compositing or detailed colour work.
  4. Audio requirements: Dialogue cleanup, noise reduction, syncing separate audio and sound design affect effort.
  5. Captions and graphics: Subtitles, animated titles, product callouts and branded templates add work.
  6. Deadline and revisions: Rush delivery and repeated changes can change the project scope.
  7. Output formats: One horizontal master is different from multiple vertical and square versions.

Typical project types

Instagram Reels and Shorts

Usually involve selecting the strongest moments, shaping a clear opening, pacing cuts, adding readable captions when required and exporting in vertical format. Share the platform and target duration before work starts.

YouTube videos

Long-form edits may include footage organisation, story flow, audio balancing, B-roll, titles, basic colour correction and a final quality check. A talking-head tutorial and a multicamera interview have different requirements.

Business promotional videos

These should communicate one clear customer benefit. The edit may combine product or service visuals, staff or location shots, voice-over, text overlays and a call to action. A short brief helps prevent an attractive video from missing its business purpose.

Checklist: what to send your video editor

  • Raw footage link and approximate total duration.
  • Desired final duration and platform (Instagram, YouTube, website, etc.).
  • One or two reference videos that explain the style you want.
  • Correct logo, brand colours, text and spelling of names.
  • Music or voice-over files, with usage rights confirmed.
  • Required captions, language and aspect ratios.
  • Deadline, number of deliverables and who will approve the edit.
  • Any must-use shots and moments that must not be included.

Questions to ask before confirming a quotation

  • What exactly is included in the edit?
  • Are captions, stock assets, motion graphics or audio cleanup included?
  • How many correction rounds are included?
  • What is the expected first-preview and final-delivery timeline?
  • How should large files be shared and stored?

Example: prepare a clear editing brief

Suppose you have recorded a 25-minute interview and need one 8–10 minute YouTube edit plus three short vertical clips. Before requesting a quote, tell the editor whether the camera and external audio are already synchronised, identify the important sections, share the guest name and approved lower-third text, and explain whether the three clips need separate captions. This gives both sides a shared understanding of the deliverables. It is an illustrative brief, not a fixed package or price.

How to review an editor’s first draft

  • Check that the opening quickly explains what the video is about.
  • Listen on headphones for dialogue level changes, clipping and distracting background noise.
  • Check names, numbers, logos and on-screen text carefully.
  • Watch the full export once on a phone to confirm captions and framing are readable.
  • Confirm the file format, resolution and aspect ratio match the intended platform.

How to avoid common project delays

Send all footage in clearly named folders, avoid sending compressed copies when original files are available, provide one consolidated feedback list and confirm the final script or on-screen text. If multiple people are reviewing, appoint one person to collect feedback. Confirm that you have permission to use supplied footage, logos, music and images.

Video Editing ka budget kaise plan karein?

Editing ka budget decide karte waqt sirf final video ki length mat dekhiye. Editor ko kitna raw footage review karna hai, kitne camera angles hain, audio clean karna hai ya nahi, captions aur motion graphics chahiye ya nahi—ye sab effort ko affect karte hain. Isliye quotation compare karne se pehle same brief sabhi editors ko bhejein. Tab aap apples-to-apples comparison kar paayenge.

Example: ek business video project

Maan lijiye aapke paas 40 minutes ka interview footage hai aur aapko ek 6-minute YouTube video ke saath 3 vertical Reels chahiye. Brief mein final duration, aspect ratio, subtitles, brand logo, music rights, delivery date aur revision rounds clearly mention karein. Agar ye details pehle clear hongi, toh project ke beech mein extra cost ya delay ki possibility kam hogi.

Professional editor ko files kaise hand over karein?

Footage ko folders mein arrange karein: Camera A, Camera B, Audio, Logo & Brand Assets, Reference Videos aur Final Text. File names meaningful rakhein aur ek short note dein ki kaunse moments important hain. Raw footage ka backup apne paas zaroor rakhein. Final delivery ke baad export ko phone aur laptop dono par check karein—captions, spelling, audio aur framing specially verify karein.

Quick tip: Clear brief, organised footage aur timely feedback editing ko fast aur professional banate hain.

Frequently asked questions

Is video editing charged per minute?

Some editors quote by final video length, while others quote by project, footage volume or editing complexity. Ask what the quote includes instead of comparing only a per-minute figure.

Can I hire an editor if I have already recorded the video?

Yes. Share the original footage, audio files, intended platform, desired duration and reference style to check whether the material is suitable for editing.

Does a Reels edit include captions and music?

Not automatically. Confirm captions, music selection, sound effects, graphics and revision rounds in the written scope.
