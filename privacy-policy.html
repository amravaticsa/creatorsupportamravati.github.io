Privacy Policy

How Creator Support Amravati handles website enquiries and analytics information.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Creator Support Amravati (CSA), based in Amravati, Maharashtra, handles information when you visit creatorsupportamravati.in or contact us about our services.

Information you provide

You may choose to provide your name, phone number, email address, business details and project requirements when contacting us by phone, email or enquiry form. Our enquiry form is hosted by Google Forms. Information submitted through that form is processed by Google under its own terms and privacy practices.

Payments and order details

If you choose to use the secure payment page after receiving a CSA quotation or invoice, the page asks for the approved project amount, payment option (advance or full payment), your name, email address and mobile/WhatsApp number. You may separately opt in to receive order and payment updates on WhatsApp. The website sends these order details and your opt-in choice to CSA's payment API hosted on Cloudflare Workers to create the payment order and request payment verification. Razorpay Checkout processes the payment and handles the payment credentials under Razorpay's own terms and privacy practices. CSA's website form does not ask you to enter card or UPI credentials directly into CSA-controlled fields. CSA receives payment/order information, including a payment ID and verification result, to reconcile the payment with your quotation or invoice. Use the payment page only for the amount approved by CSA.

Website analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website, such as which pages are visited and how visitors interact with the site. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features or measurement.

Advertising cookies and third-party vendors

If Google AdSense or other advertising services are enabled on this website, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Users may review or change Google's advertising personalisation settings at Google Ads Settings. Users can also learn about how Google uses information from partner sites at Google's partner-site information page. More information about Google's advertising technologies and controls is available in Google's advertising policies and technologies.

If advertising is enabled for visitors in regions such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, CSA will configure a Google-certified consent management platform where required before using advertising technologies that need consent. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Google sign-in and article likes

Some article pages offer an optional Like feature and a Share control. If you choose to Like an article, Google sign-in is used through Firebase Authentication. Firebase may process your Google account information to authenticate you. CSA stores a Firebase user identifier and the like timestamp in Firebase Firestore so that a signed-in user can add or remove their Like and the page can show an aggregate Like count. Your email address and Google profile details are not displayed as a public comment or review by this feature.

Article sharing uses your device or browser's native share or copy function. CSA does not operate a public comment form at this time. Do not send sensitive information through any third-party share or sign-in service.

Website presence and visit statistics

CSA uses Firebase Realtime Database to show an approximate live visitor count and maintain aggregate website usage counters, including daily visits, lifetime visits, total page views and page views by page path. A temporary session record is created while a browser is connected and is configured for removal when that connection ends. Session storage in the browser helps avoid counting some repeat visits more than once in the same session. These counters are used for general site measurement and are not intended to identify individual visitors.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss requested services
  • To prepare quotations and coordinate agreed projects
  • To maintain, secure and improve this website and our services
  • To understand general website traffic and advertising performance
  • To meet applicable legal, accounting and security requirements

Sharing and service providers

CSA does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers involved in operating this website or services you choose to use, including Google Forms, Google Analytics, Google Firebase Authentication, Firebase Firestore (for article likes) and Firebase Realtime Database (for aggregate presence and visit statistics), Cloudflare Workers (for the payment API), Razorpay (for secure payment processing), and, if enabled, Google advertising services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights and users.

Data retention and security

We retain enquiry and project information for as long as reasonably necessary for communication, service delivery, accounting, record-keeping or legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements. You can manage cookies in your browser and manage Google's advertising personalisation through the settings linked above. You may also stop using the engagement features and sign out of your Google session on the website.

Children's privacy

This website and our services are intended for a general audience and are not knowingly directed at children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information to us.
