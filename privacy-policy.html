Privacy Policy

How Creator Support Amravati handles website enquiries and analytics information.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Creator Support Amravati (CSA) may handle information when you visit this website or contact us about our services.

Information you provide

If you contact us by phone, email or our enquiry form, you may provide details such as your name, phone number, email address, business information and project requirements. The enquiry form is hosted by Google Forms; information submitted through it is also subject to Google’s applicable terms and privacy practices.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage, such as pages visited and interactions. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Google’s own privacy terms also apply to its services.

How information may be used

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss requested services
  • To prepare quotations and coordinate projects
  • To maintain and improve the website and services
  • To address support, security or legal requirements

Sharing

CSA does not offer personal information for sale. Information may be processed by service providers you choose to use when interacting with this website, such as Google Forms and Google Analytics, or disclosed where required by applicable law.

Retention and security

We retain enquiry and project information for as long as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, records or legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and contact

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements. Email amravati.csa@gmail.com with privacy-related questions.
